# Detail library: prompts for ChatGPT (images by William)

Vertical photos (9:16) of the details Tesla owners know by heart. Made once, checked by William, then used
automatically in Shorts and long videos (`video/story.py`, story beats with `"lib": ["<name>.jpg"]`).

**How to deliver:** save each image as JPG with exactly the file name in the heading, and upload them on GitHub:
https://github.com/tbeugelink/trendingtesla/upload/main/static/img/lib (drag the files in, then "Commit changes").
Tip: give ChatGPT one real reference photo of the part (Tesla press photo or your own) together with the prompt.

**Paste this first line before every prompt:**

> Photorealistic editorial photo, VERTICAL 9:16 (1080x1920), natural light, sharp focus, realistic proportions, colourful but natural colours with small yellow, red and blue accents in the surroundings (never grey or washed out). The main subject large and sharp in the centre third of the frame. No readable text anywhere, no licence plates, no recognisable faces, no logos except the real Tesla badges.
