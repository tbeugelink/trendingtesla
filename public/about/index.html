About TrendingTesla

TrendingTesla follows everything that moves at Tesla: vehicles, software, self-driving, robotaxi, Optimus, energy and the company's results, the wider ecosystem of SpaceX, Starlink, The Boring Company and xAI where it touches Tesla, plus honest buying advice for Tesla owners.

Several times a day we pick the stories that matter most and explain them in plain language: what happened, why it matters and what to watch next. We write our own articles and always link to the sources we used, so you can check them.

Articles are researched and written with the help of AI and published under editorial supervision. Spotted a mistake? We correct it as soon as we know about it.
