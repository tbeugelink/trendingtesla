Tesla Model Y Standard

Tesla Model Y Standard

The cheaper Model Y, launched in October 2025 with less equipment and a lower price.

Specifications

PriceUK from £41,990 · Germany from €39,990
Battery typeLithium-ion; chemistry differs by factory (LFP or nickel-based)
Rangeup to 505 km WLTP
Driverear-wheel drive
Wheels18-inch
DC chargingup to 225 kW
Roofglass roof covered on the inside
Suspensionpassive dampers

Points to watch

  • Missing compared with Premium: e.g. ventilated seats, rear screen, ambient lighting, power-folding mirrors (varies by market).
  • In North America Autopilot is limited to traffic-aware cruise control.
  • Many Premium accessories don't fit; check compatibility.

What makes it special

  • The cheapest new Model Y.
  • Same space and Supercharger access as the other versions.
