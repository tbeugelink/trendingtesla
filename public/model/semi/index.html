Tesla Semi

Tesla's electric heavy truck, in series production in Nevada since 2026.

Specifications

BuiltNevada; series production since 2026
Body2-door heavy tractor (Class 8), central driver's seat
Drivethree electric motors
Power800 kW (about 1,070 hp)
Accelerationnot applicable
Top speednot officially stated
Battery548 kWh (Standard) · 822 kWh (Long Range), per CARB filing
Battery type (maker)4680 cells, NCMA — Tesla (own cells)
Range325 miles / 520 km (Standard) · 500 miles / 800 km (Long Range)
Consumption106.0 kWh/100 km = 0.9 km/kWh (≈ 1 in 9 km per litre of diesel), Tesla: 1.7 kWh per mile
ChargingMegacharger up to 1.2 MW
Fast-charge time (10–80%)most of the range in about 30 min at a Megacharger (Tesla)
Weighttractor under 20,000 lb / 9 t (Standard) · about 23,000 lb / 10.4 t (Long Range)
Towinggross combination weight 82,000 lb (37 t)
Steeringround wheel, central driving position; conventional steering
Autopilot / FSDEnhanced Autopilot (lane keeping, adaptive cruise, emergency braking); no FSD announced for customers
Glass roofno
Price newabout $260,000 (Standard) · $290,000 (Long Range), reported
Price now (used)not applicable

Points to watch

  • Needs Megacharger access along the routes.

What makes it special

  • Central driving position.
  • Most of the range back in about 30 minutes at a Megacharger.
  • Factory: Nevada, up to 50,000 trucks a year
