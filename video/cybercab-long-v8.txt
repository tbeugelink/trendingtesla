TITLE:
Start Model 2 in december!

DESCRIPTION:
On 13 November 2026, Tesla's robotaxi account invited select customers to purchase Cybercab for personal use. The order page showed $29,990 before tax, a $1,000 deposit, and a $299 a month autonomy subscription, with delivery estimated for December 2026 in Austin.

Elon Musk replied that the monthly fee is too high and should be closer to about $115 a month.

The Verge, Axios, and TechCrunch, citing the order terms, reported that the buyer may not use the car commercially. That limit is reported from those outlets. Tesla has not called this car Model 2.
